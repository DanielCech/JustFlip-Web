::: lede
JustFlip! looks simple on purpose: a card, a flip, a grade. Underneath
are fill-in-the-blank cards, typeset maths, diagrams, spoken hints, Anki
import, scheduling priorities, a Watch app and a lot more. This manual
walks through all of it — and through the small rules that are easy to
trip over.
:::
