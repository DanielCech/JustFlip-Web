An honest comparison

JustFlip vs Anki:
which one should you use?

We make JustFlip, so read this with that in mind. We’ve tried to be fair: where Anki is the better choice, we say so, and every fact about Anki links to its source.

In one line

Anki is the free, open, endlessly customisable standard. JustFlip is Anki-style scheduling rebuilt as a native Apple app, with a hold button that never touches your memory data.

01

What’s the short answer?

If you want free, cross-platform, endlessly customisable spaced repetition, and you’re on Windows, Linux or Android, use Anki. It is excellent, and on desktop and Android it costs nothing.

If you study mostly on Apple devices and want the same kind of scheduling (SM-2 or FSRS, with the interval shown on every grade button) in an app that feels native on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, JustFlip is built for that. Your Anki decks import.

02

What does Anki do better?

Price on most platforms. Anki desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux) and AnkiDroid are free, and so is AnkiWeb sync (apps.ankiweb.net).

Every platform. Anki runs on Windows, Linux and Android. JustFlip is Apple-only and has no plans for Android or web.

Ecosystem. Anki has add-ons and a large library of shared decks built over many years. JustFlip has no deck sharing between users yet, only import/export and a small curated catalogue.

Depth of customisation. Anki’s note types, card templates and CSS go further than any other flashcard app. JustFlip’s Anki import handles images, formulas, code, emphasis and legacy Image Occlusion decks, but unusual note types may still come across imperfectly.

Open source, with a long track record.

03

What does JustFlip do differently?

The same visible scheduling Anki users expect. Like Anki (Anki manual), every grade button shows the interval it will produce before you press it. SM-2 by default, FSRS-5 as a Pro option (“Smart schedule (beta)”).

Postpone without lying to the algorithm. Put a card, deck or whole interest on hold (tomorrow, 3 days, 1 week or custom) without touching its scheduling data. There’s no ease change and no lapse, so you never have to press “Again” on a card you simply weren’t ready for.

Native on every Apple device. iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, with a home-screen widget showing your due count, Siri Shortcuts (“How many cards are due?” answers without opening the app), and Spotlight search of your cards.

No account. iCloud sync is included free, with no sign-up. It works fully offline. No ads.

Your Anki decks come with you. .apkg import keeps images, formulas, code, emphasis and legacy Image Occlusion decks, and there’s CSV import/export plus a one-file backup.

Can I move my Anki decks to JustFlip?

Yes. Export an .apkg from Anki and open it in JustFlip. Images, formulas, code, emphasis and legacy Image Occlusion decks come across. Unusual note types may not come across perfectly, so check a few cards after the import. CSV works too.

06

Is JustFlip free?

Yes, for up to 3 interests with 5 decks each, with no time limit. Pro adds background listening, advanced statistics, progress trackers and Ask AI for a whole deck (Ask AI on a single card is free). There’s a 7-day in-app Pro trial. When it ends, nothing you made is locked away.

Does it use AI to write your cards? No. “Ask AI” builds a prompt from a card or deck for the chat AI you already use (ChatGPT, Claude, …), and “Paste cards” imports its answer. The app itself calls no AI service.

07

Who should pick which?

Pick Anki if you use Windows, Linux or Android, rely on add-ons or community decks, want to heavily customise card templates, or want to pay nothing.

Pick JustFlip if you study on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, want to postpone cards without disturbing your schedule, and would rather not manage an account.

Or use both: keep Anki on your PC and try JustFlip on your phone. The free tier is enough to try it with a few imported decks.

Frequently asked questions.

Is JustFlip made by the Anki team?

No. JustFlip is an independent app by Daniel Čech, an iOS engineer in Prague. It imports Anki’s .apkg format but isn’t affiliated with Anki.

Does JustFlip support FSRS?

Yes. FSRS-5 is available as a Pro option called “Smart schedule (beta)”. SM-2 is the default.

Does JustFlip work on Android or Windows?

No. JustFlip is Apple-only (iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch), by choice, and there are no plans for Android or web.

Can I import my Anki decks?

Yes, as .apkg files. Images, formulas, code, emphasis and legacy Image Occlusion decks are kept. Unusual note types may come across imperfectly.

Do I need an account?

No. Sync runs through your own iCloud, and the app works fully offline.

How much does JustFlip cost?

A free tier of 3 interests × 5 decks with no time limit. Pro is $3.99/month, $19.99/year or $24.99 lifetime (US prices; other storefronts use Apple’s local equivalents).
