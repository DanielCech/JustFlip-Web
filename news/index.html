What's new

Fresh in JustFlip

The newest features, release by release — from a Today screen that flips like a card to diagrams, tags and beautifully typeset maths.

  1. Latest

    Version 1.3.0

    Today flips like a flashcard

    The Today panel now has two sides. The front takes you straight into your review. Flip it to see today's progress, your best streak and a two-week timeline of past and upcoming reviews.

    • One tap to start reviewing
    • Keep going when today's goal is done but cards are still due

    Your Anki decks, all of them

    Decks from current versions of Anki now come across faithfully — image occlusion, cloze cards, type-in answers, maths, chemistry, furigana and code included.

    • Importing a course? JustFlip suggests an interest for it and keeps deck names short
    • Import a deck again to update it, or skip it

    Card text at your size

    Give your cards their own text size, separate from the rest of the app. In Settings, turn off Card text follows app text size and pick the size that reads best.

    • Pictures sit neatly under the text
    • Tap a picture to zoom in

    A roomier Mac

    On Mac, JustFlip now has the same two-column layout as iPad: interests and decks in the sidebar, your cards right beside them.

    • The sidebar stays put however you size the window
    • Welcome screens fit the window

    Also new: headings in a fresh typeface, smoother image occlusion and plenty of small fixes.

  2. Earlier release

    Version 1.2.0

    Mermaid diagrams

    Write a ```mermaid code block on a card and JustFlip draws the diagram for you — flowcharts, sequence diagrams and more. It is the quickest way to learn processes, architectures and relationships that are easier to see than to read.

    • Flowcharts, sequence diagrams and more, drawn from plain text
    • Renders in light and dark, on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch
    • Editor help included, so you can start from a working example

  3. Earlier release

    Version 1.1.x

    Tags and colour flags

    Organise cards your own way. Add tags right in the card editor, and flag cards with a colour so the important ones stand out when you browse a deck.

    • Tag cards with a chip field in the card editor
    • Flag cards with a colour to make them stand out
    • Find the cards you are looking for faster

  4. Earlier release

    Version 1.0.x

    TeX maths

    Write $inline$ and $$display$$ maths using familiar TeX syntax. Formulas render crisply on your cards, at any size.

    • Inline and display maths with standard TeX syntax
    • Crisp, typeset formulas right on the card
    • Great for maths, physics and chemistry
