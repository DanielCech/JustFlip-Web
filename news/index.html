What's new

Fresh in JustFlip

The newest features, release by release — from diagrams on your cards to tags, colour flags and beautifully typeset maths.

  1. Latest

    Version 1.2.0

    Mermaid diagrams

    Write a ```mermaid code block on a card and JustFlip draws the diagram for you — flowcharts, sequence diagrams and more. It is the quickest way to learn processes, architectures and relationships that are easier to see than to read.

    • Flowcharts, sequence diagrams and more, drawn from plain text
    • Renders in light and dark, on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch
    • Editor help included, so you can start from a working example

  2. Earlier release

    Version 1.1.x

    Tags and colour flags

    Organise cards your own way. Add tags right in the card editor, and flag cards with a colour so the important ones stand out when you browse a deck.

    • Tag cards with a chip field in the card editor
    • Flag cards with a colour to make them stand out
    • Find the cards you are looking for faster

  3. Earlier release

    Version 1.0.x

    TeX maths

    Write $inline$ and $$display$$ maths using familiar TeX syntax. Formulas render crisply on your cards, at any size.

    • Inline and display maths with standard TeX syntax
    • Crisp, typeset formulas right on the card
    • Great for maths, physics and chemistry
